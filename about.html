About me

Who I am

My work has moved across political consultancy, public education systems, and emerging technology, but one question has stayed constant. Who gets reached by the systems we build, and who does not?

I am pursuing a master's in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship at UNC-Chapel Hill. I am also a Class 24 Rotary Peace Fellow at the Duke-UNC Rotary Peace Center and a Carolina Diplomacy Fellow. Before coming to North Carolina, I studied political science at the University of Delhi and international relations at Jawaharlal Nehru University.

What I do

I began my career in political consultancy on the Uttar Pradesh state assembly elections, then moved into education. I spent nearly six years at NewGlobe Education, progressing from associate to deputy managing editor of instructional design and working on government education programs across Africa and India. I led the homework books project, co-authoring 10 books and proposing QR-linked audio for phonics practice. I piloted the first few homeworks from two volumes with students in Nigeria and received feedback from both students and teachers. During the 2023 Manipur crisis, I helped get textbooks to children whose schooling had been disrupted.

My work now focuses on learning and governance in the age of AI. As an intern at UNICEF's Office of Internal Audit and Investigations, I am gaining a closer view of how an international organization approaches evidence, accountability, due process, and the use of AI tools. At UNC, I am also part of the ethics group of a student AI committee exploring responsible approaches to AI in higher education.

How I approach it

I tend to look at systems from the last mile inward. That means asking who can actually access an intervention, whose realities shaped its design, and who may fall outside the assumptions built into it. I learn from the people closest to a problem, test ideas before scaling them, and use evidence and feedback to decide what comes next.

Where I am headed

I am working toward a role in AI governance and education policy at a multilateral institution. As AI becomes part of how people learn, work, and access public systems, I want to help communities furthest from new technologies take part in that transition.
