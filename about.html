About me

I am an education and AI governance professional with nearly six years of experience in government education programs across Africa and India, now working on how AI reaches children in underserved communities.

I work in English and Hindi.

Who gets reached

My work has moved across political consultancy, public education systems, and emerging technology, but one question has stayed constant. Who gets reached by the systems we build, and who does not?

I am pursuing a master's in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship at UNC-Chapel Hill. I am also a Class 24 Rotary Peace Fellow at the Duke-UNC Rotary Peace Center and a Carolina Diplomacy Fellow. Before coming to North Carolina, I studied political science at the University of Delhi and international relations at Jawaharlal Nehru University.

What I do now

My work now focuses on how AI is governed and who it serves. As an intern at UNICEF's Office of Internal Audit and Investigations, I am gaining a closer view of how an international organization approaches evidence, accountability, due process, and the use of AI tools. At UNC, I am also part of the ethics group of a student AI committee exploring responsible approaches to AI in higher education.

Where I have worked

Before graduate school, I began my career in political consultancy on the Uttar Pradesh state assembly elections, then moved into education. I spent nearly six years at NewGlobe Education, progressing from associate to deputy managing editor of instructional design
