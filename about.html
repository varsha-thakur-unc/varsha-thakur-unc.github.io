About me

I'm a second-year graduate student at the UNC-Chapel Hill School of Education, pursuing a Master's in Educational Innovation, Technology, and Entrepreneurship with a concentration in Innovation Specialist. I'm also a Class 24 Rotary Peace Fellow at the Duke-UNC Rotary Peace Center and a Carolina Diplomacy Fellow.

My academic background spans political science at the University of Delhi and international relations at Jawaharlal Nehru University. Before graduate school, I developed learning materials for RwandaEQUIP and government education programs across West Africa, and worked as a junior associate on state assembly elections in India.

My work sits at the intersection of AI governance, digital safety for children in underserved communities, conflict-affected and displacement settings. I am working toward a career in AI governance, knowledge building, multilateral policy, or UN-adjacent institutional work.

I keep returning to one question. As AI moves into classrooms and humanitarian systems, who benefits, who gets left behind, and how can we close that gap?
